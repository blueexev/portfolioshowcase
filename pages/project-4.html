“How might we turn Indian folktales into an interactive experience where children don’t just hear the story they build, shape and perform it?”

Context

Inspired by India’s deep-rooted storytelling traditions, ranging from folk theatre and puppetry to oral narratives passed down through generations, I created Iti as a way to help children reconnect with this rich cultural heritage in a contemporary format.

India is known for its diverse traditional art forms like puppetry, folklore, and regional storytelling practices, but many of these are slowly fading in today’s digital age.

The Problem

Children often experience stories by simply listening or watching. I wanted to explore how storytelling could become something they build, touch, change and perform themselves.

The Game

Iti is an interactive storytelling game and DIY theatre kit designed to help children rediscover India’s cultural roots through folktales and performance. Blending craft, play and technology, Iti encourages young minds to create, imagine and connect with traditional stories in a modern way.

Each child becomes both a creator and performer, building their own miniature puppet theatre, crafting characters, exploring them in 3D through AR and finally bringing the tale to life through a dramatic act. An added AR layer allows them to bring their handmade puppets to life in 3D, blending traditional storytelling with digital wonder.

Inspiration

Drawing from traditional Indian puppet theatre, especially forms like shadow puppetry and string puppetry, the mood is rooted in drama, folklore and handcrafted aesthetics. The stage compositions, rich textiles and ornamental frames reflect the essence of live folk performances, where storytelling unfolds through movement, light and silhouette.

Experience of the gameplay

Make: Children build their personal mini theatre using pre-cut materials from the kit.

Create: They design and decorate character puppets inspired by folktales.

Perform: Enact the story as a full theatre act, learning moral lessons, emotions and cultural nuances through play.

Discover: Using AR, they scan activation cards to explore their characters coming to life.

What is inside the kit?

DIY Theatre Kit: Pre-cut materials and assembly guides to build a personal mini stage.

Character Templates: Blank puppets and cutouts to design, decorate and personalize.

Cultural Insight Cards: Fun facts and short learning prompts about regions and folklore origins.

Story Flashcards: Traditional Indian folktales broken down into acts and dialogues.

Instruction Booklet: Step-by-step guide for setup and performance.

AR Activation Cards: Scan to view your characters come alive in 3D through an AR feature.

Making storytelling interactive

Instead of giving children one fixed way to tell the story, Iti gives them pieces to experiment with. They can choose characters, arrange the stage, use dialogue cards and perform the story in their own way.

The AR layer adds another dimension to physical play. Children can scan elements of the kit to discover additional parts of the story, making the physical theatre feel like it has another hidden layer.

Iti turns storytelling into a shared act of making and performing. Children build something together, give characters a voice and become part of the story instead of simply watching it.
