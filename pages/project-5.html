Overview

Many Eyes is an interactive AR installation built around a single postcard. Visitors scan the postcard, and a 3D model appears. They choose an option, scan framed images around the space, and see content that matches their choice. They can then leave their own perspective as text, a voice note, or a photo of something they have drawn or made. Each response appears at a random position around the image and stays there permanently. Every new visitor sees what came before and adds to it.

Concept

An image rarely has one meaning. A Buddha head held in tree roots can be read as nature preserving a sacred object or as nature swallowing it. A protest can be read as courage or as disruption. Many Eyes makes this difference visible. It treats each image as an open question and collects the answers in the same space.

The postcard is a physical object anyone can pick up. The AR layer grows with every visitor. The installation is never finished and its feed is made entirely of the people who passed through.

The Experience

Scan the postcard with the app.
A 3D model rises from the card.
Choose an option and tap OK.
Scan the frames around the space.
Only the frame matching your option plays its image or video.
Record your perspective as text or a photo of a drawing.
Submit. Your response appears at a random spot around the frame.
The next visitor sees yours and adds theirs.

Interface
