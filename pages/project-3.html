Context

Ahmedabad today exists as a collection of fragmented ecological islands. Famous green lungs like Parimal Garden and the Riverfront are majestic in isolation but they are severed by vast, hostile expanses of heat and toxic air.

While our urban planning has successfully created a seamless network for human transit and concrete density, it has inadvertently constructed a series of “Biological Deserts” for the wildlife that sustains our ecosystem.

For a bird or a pollinator, the city is not a grid of roads; it is a landscape of impassable Chemical Walls where high AQI and extreme thermal stress act as invisible barriers, trapping life in shrinking pockets of green.

Problem Statement

This project focuses on birds in Ahmedabad. Through data visualization, the project highlights how poor air quality, especially PM2.5 pollution caused by urban construction affects bird health and disrupts their natural habitat and survival.

Target Audience

Architects

This data prompts architects and urban planners to advocate for inserting green bubbles every 500 meters as an ecological requirement. Our target audience are the designers of the city who can make an actual impact.

To move away from “anthropocentric design,” the shift must happen at the planning level.

Planners are the ones who can transform Ahmedabad from a series of disconnected parks into a breathable, living network.

They professionals responsible for essential life support infrastructure this argues that biological connectivity is a fundamental part of that urban utility.

Dataset and methodology

  • The physical map of city of Ahmedabad
  • The AQI data over the past 3 years (2022–2024)
  • The body mass of specific bird species
  • Locations of different talukas and important places in (x,y) format
  • AQI data over the city (most and least affected)

Final Experience

Major Components of our project

Loading screen

Loading Screen

User selects a bird species. Corresponding habitat and AQI map load.

Bird simulation

Bird Simulation

Birds move as a flock using noise-based motion, cohesion behavior and collision avoidance.

AQI detection

AQI Detection

Each bird samples AQI map colors. Pollution severity is classified into different colours.

Health system

Health System

Three sliders decrease based on pollution: Lung Damage, Wing Damage, Neurological Damage.

Path visualization

Path Visualization

After extinction: bird movement paths can be displayed. User can export path image. User can restart.

Weekly progression

Weekly Progression

Every 10 seconds = 1 week. Environmental warnings appear. Birds gradually die over time.

Goal of the project

The goal is to generate a map that identifies “connectivity gaps” where wildlife is being throttled.

The goal of the project is to prompt urban architects and planners to create ecological spaces and bubbles for birds to cope with bad AQI levels. It also adds value if people are more aware of their actions and its effect.
